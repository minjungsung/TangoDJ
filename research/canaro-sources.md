# Canaro recording sources

The sources below are YouTube upload titles and descriptions collected on 2026-10-15. They establish the selected video identity and reported recording credits; they do not constitute independent discographic verification. Modern album release dates and copyright years are not treated as recording dates. Energy labels are editorial selections pending full listening review.
